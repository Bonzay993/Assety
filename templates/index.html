Frequently Asked Questions

What can I track?

Record equipment and other company assets, including tags, serial numbers, categories, locations, purchase costs, warranty dates, and notes.

How do I get started?

Create an account, add a category and an optional location, then add your first asset.

Can I export my records?

Yes. Reports provides CSV and PDF downloads. Logs can also be exported as PDF.

Can I use Assety on my phone?

Yes. The interface adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Who can see my assets?

Sign-in is required for inventory pages. Each account has a separate company workspace, and the application checks access to records and images.

Ready to organise your assets?

Create an account to start your inventory, or sign in to your existing workspace.
